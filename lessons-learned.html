Lessons Learned

What worked

Meeting across disciplines. Participants valued the mix of disciplines, open discussion, and opportunities to meet people outside their usual professional circles.

Volunteer support. Participants praised the volunteers’ contribution while identifying a need for stronger preparation and coordination.

FeedbackResponse
Scheduling and navigation. Overlapping sessions made it difficult to choose what to attend. Finding rooms and identifying speakers also needed attention.KoD introduced clearer signage and session indicators for the 2025 edition. The subsequent review still called for fewer scheduling conflicts and clearer routes between venues.
Sound and seating. Reviews raised seating and speaker visibility. The makers’ lab later needed stronger sound support, microphones, and more comfortable seating for popular sessions.Dedicated speaking setups were introduced for 2025. Later recommendations called for checking room capacity, audio, microphones, and seating before confirming the schedule.
Workshops and session formats. Participants wanted more structure in workshops and clearer session formats. Later discussions proposed demonstrations and project examples alongside presentations.KoD introduced structured masterclasses with external facilitators for 2025. Demonstrations and project-based formats remain proposals for future programs.
Volunteers and partner promotion. Volunteers needed clearer roles, training, and coordination. Earlier promotion shared across program partners was also requested.Recommendations included defining volunteer responsibilities and agreeing on promotion responsibilities and dates with partners.
Mentoring and participation. Contributors proposed mentoring, greater participation by less familiar local voices, and opportunities for students and early-career designers.The proposal requires defining how mentoring would work, who could participate, and what support would continue afterward.
Documentation and follow-up. Participants wanted ways to maintain connections and continue discussions after the event. Contributors also raised missing recordings and the need for reliable notes and reports.Recommendations included a post-event summary, selected recordings, follow-up activities, clear documentation responsibilities, and permission checks before sharing.
