Lessons Learned

Program reviews, participant feedback, and decisions about future Knowledge of Design activities.

Why this record exists

What we learned

Feedback has value when we record it accurately and return to it when planning the next edition.

KoD has held discussions with previous speakers, staff, collaborators, and invited specialists. The notes below distinguish their observations from proposals and changes that still need confirmation. They do not represent a participant survey or a measurement of outcomes.

18 November 2025

Event review

A recorded review following the Riyadh edition included organizers, colleagues, and KPMG representatives. The discussion covered the program, speaker engagement, volunteers, sound, seating, promotion, and what participants might take away after attending.

Scheduling and navigation

Parallel sessions

Participants reported that overlapping talks made it difficult to choose what to attend. Some preferred a simpler schedule.

Proposed response

Review the number of concurrent sessions and make routes between venues and schedules clearer.

Status: Recommendation recorded

Venue and equipment

Sound and seating

The makers' lab needed stronger sound support and more comfortable seating for popular sessions. Microphone provision was also raised.

Proposed response

Check room capacity, audio requirements, microphones, and seating before confirming the schedule.

Status: Recommendation recorded

Volunteers and promotion

Preparation

Participants praised the contribution of volunteers and asked for stronger training and coordination. Earlier, shared promotion by program partners was also discussed.

Proposed response

Clarify volunteer roles and responsibilities, and agree on promotion responsibilities and dates with partners.

Status: Recommendation recorded

After the event

Keeping conversations going

Participants wanted clearer follow-up and ways to capture the questions raised in sessions rather than ending the exchange when the event finished.

Proposed response

Plan a post-event summary, document selected discussions, and identify follow-up activities.

Status: Recommendation recorded

1 September 2026

Speaker discussion

Former speakers, invited contributors, and partners, including a KPMG representative, discussed the third edition and possible activities afterward. Suggestions included sessions with clearer subjects, demonstrations, mentoring, and better ways to document what participants learned.

Session format

Talks and demonstrations

Participants proposed combining discussion with practical demonstrations and project examples, rather than relying only on presentations.

Next consideration

Test formats that allow people to see methods, ask questions, and examine results.

Status: Proposal discussed

Young designers

Access to experienced people

Mentoring and participation by less familiar local voices were proposed, along with opportunities for students and early career designers.

Next consideration

Define what mentoring would involve, how people could participate, and how support would continue afterward.

Status: Proposal discussed

Documentation

Recording what was learned

Contributors raised concerns about missing recordings and the need for more reliable notes, reports, and follow-up.

Next consideration

Assign responsibility for documentation before sessions and check what can be shared after reviewing permissions.

Status: Proposal discussed

Reading the record

How changes are recorded

Recommendation recorded means somebody raised a concern and proposed a response. Proposal discussed means a possible future activity was considered. Completed will be used only when later documentation confirms a change was carried out. The records above make no claim that a recommendation has already been implemented.

Based on KoD's recorded November 2025 event review and September 2026 speaker discussion. Personal details, unpublished transcripts, and internal planning materials remain private.
