The Project Before the Project

Designing Cultural Infrastructure for an Age of Acceleration

About the session

Before the brief becomes fixed

Digital tools have transformed how people reach culture and information, raising new questions about what physical space can uniquely provide: context, encounter, participation, and sustained focus.

The session explores two linked ideas. The project before the project considers the research, mapping, community input, feasibility, and prototyping that help determine what should exist before an architectural brief becomes fixed. The project within the project looks at deeper material or environmental inquiry pursued through the commission itself.

Through international case studies, Ben and Theo will consider attention as a spatial resource, the difference between immersion and saturation, and how investigation can expand architectural practice from objects toward programs, networks, and longer-term cultural use.

Unknown Works

Unknown Works is a London-based architecture, design, and research studio operating internationally across cultural, commercial, scientific, workplace, community, and mixed-use projects.
