Rukun Advisory Circle

Advising the future of Knowledge of Design

The Rukun Advisory Circle brings experienced external perspective to the continuing development of Knowledge of Design. Its discussions advise the initiative's research direction, annual themes, year-round activity, publications, futures inquiry, and development as an international reference across design and STEAM.

Dr. Deaa Bataineh

Designer, researcher, advisor, and professor

Deputy Director of the Center of Excellence for Innovative Projects and Professor of Design and Innovative Strategies at Jordan University of Science and Technology, where he co-founded the design department. He holds a PhD in Design from the Institute of Design.

Selected recognition · UNDP · European Union · UN Global Compact · National Designer of Jordan · Core77 Design Awards

Dr. Gjoko Muratovski

Designer, innovation consultant, and author

Award-winning designer and innovation consultant across government advisory, higher education, and industry. Chief Innovation Officer at ICONA Design Group and previously the inaugural Ullman Endowed Chair and Director of the Ullman School of Design.

Selected affiliations · European Commission · Stanford SEED · Oxford Digital Leaders Network · White House Presidential Innovation Fellows

The Rukun Advisory Circle is separate from the edition-based Advisory Board represented elsewhere in this archive.

What design means

“The challenging part about design is that it is very broad. When I say design, it may mean something different than what is in your mind. The aim of Knowledge of Design is to broaden this perspective, to get us all into this broad looking. Design is when we create something in this life, whether it is a product, a program, an idea, or a concept.”

Participant · KoD Speaker Research 01 · 2025

“Design is not only for designers. Design is for everybody. This event created a space where neuroscientists, engineers, architects, and students could all be in the same room.”
