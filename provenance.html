Definition

A system of knowledge

Knowledge of Design is a cross-disciplinary initiative advancing design as a system of knowledge.

Drawing on science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics, KoD examines how design informs systems, environments, decisions, and how we live.

Since its founding, KoD has developed an intellectual framework, a methodology, a body of knowledge, a research agenda, an archive, and a community of contributors. Together, these position design as a lens through which relationships, behavior, culture, and the decisions and systems affecting daily life can be explored.

Its activity spans research, publications, workshops, partnerships, public programming, and an annual gathering that brings together perspectives from across sectors and areas of expertise.

Origin

Why KoD exists

KoD was founded in response to limited exchange between areas of expertise that continually influence one another.

Across science, technology, engineering, artistic practice, business, research and culture, people investigate related challenges through different methods. Knowledge of Design brings these inquiries into conversation and examines how design influences products, environments, infrastructure, services, organizations and daily experiences.

KoD creates a place for those areas to meet. Drawing on STEAM, it connects expertise, opens new lines of inquiry, and examines shared challenges, questions for future research, and the role design plays in life.

Stewardship & support

HRH Princess Noura bint Saud bin Naif Al Saud

KoD has developed with the belief, vision, and sustained support of HRH Princess Noura bint Saud bin Naif Al Saud.

As founder of Rukun and AlMashtal, she established the research, partnerships and programming through which KoD developed. Her research examines design across science, technology, engineering, arts, mathematics and cultural life.

Founder

Brandie Janow

KoD was founded by Brandie Janow, who leads its intellectual direction, research, curation, and partnerships.

The initiative developed from her conviction that design, science, and the pursuit of knowledge can inform the same inquiry, and that design can be understood not simply through what it produces, but through the knowledge, systems, relationships, and decisions it makes possible.

Institutional context

Rukun

KoD is a Rukun-led initiative.

Rukun conducts research and strategic advisory work informed by Saudi experience to cultural and economic development. It brings together research, futures thinking, strategy, and program development to create frameworks, initiatives, and systems connected to cultural and creative-sector development.

KoD brings together original studies, published material, annual programming and interdisciplinary research across design and STEAM.

Design and architecture influence material extraction, production, ecosystems and the conditions of human and nonhuman life. KoD studies these consequences through research connecting architecture, engineering, arts and science, with attention to restoration, resource use and long-term ecological effects.
