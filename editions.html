Edition One · 2024

KoD 2024

Theme: Evolving Thought, Shaping Life

On what design means

“The challenging part about design is that it is very broad. When I say design, it may mean something different than what is in your mind. The aim of Knowledge of Design is to broaden this perspective, to get us all into this broad looking. Design is when we create something in this life, whether it is a product, a program, an idea, or a concept.”

Amwaj Alzahrani

“Design is not only for designers. Design is for everybody. This event created a space where neuroscientists, engineers, architects, and students could all be in the same room.”

Rui Manuel Gonçalves

“Design is not the outcome. Design is the whole process. The outcome is the paper, the cup, the chair, the house, the tangible.”

Amwaj Alzahrani

“A meaningful design is one generated from data, from factors you are responding to. We need to speak a certain language, send a message, tell a story.”

Alwaleed Albaik

“People felt they were in a community, rather than at one of those stuffy conferences. We managed to depart from buzzwords.”

Mariana De' Carli
