About Rukun

Research, innovation, and strategy.

Rukun is a research, innovation, and strategy company.

We contribute Saudi-based insight to global conversations on culture and economic development while supporting national priorities. Our role is to design the structures behind creative sectors, from program architecture and institutional models to ecosystem frameworks and capacity systems.

We combine research, futures thinking, and program design to guide how sectors are structured and developed, translating ambition into frameworks, initiatives, and systems that function in practice.

Research & Knowledge Assets

Frameworks, future signals, reference models, and authored materials that inform cultural policy and innovation pathways.

Program Architecture

Structure, outcomes, and continuity for national programs and institutions, including the Craft Houses Initiative by the Heritage Commission.

Knowledge of Design

Aligned with Rukun's intellectual direction, a public forum for inquiry connected to a broader body of research.

Stewardship & institutional

Partnership pathways

Host Organization

Creates, operates, and stewards the platform.

Founding Partner

Provides strategic direction, oversees operations, and sets the future direction. Supported the platform in its early stages and contributed to its establishment, recognised for lasting commitment.

Strategic Partner

Brings expertise, influence, networks, or institutional support to advance broader objectives and continued growth.

Principal Partner

A major supporter contributing significant resources, infrastructure, and financial backing.

Institutional Partner

Formally aligned through shared objectives. May include universities, museums, foundations, commissions, and councils.

Knowledge & programming

Knowledge Partner

Contributes thought leadership and specialist knowledge, supporting talks, discussions, publications, and educational programming.

Research Partner

Contributes to formal research, studies, data, and analysis, helping generate evidence and new understanding.

Academic Partner

An educational institution contributing faculty expertise, student engagement, and academic programming.

Program Partner

Contributes directly to programming: workshops, exhibitions, discussions, and public activities.

Cultural Partner

Contributes cultural expertise, collections, heritage, and specialist knowledge of the creative industries.

Industry Partner

Represents a professional sector, contributing sector expertise, professional perspectives, and practical insight.

Reach, support & people

Community Partner

Supports outreach, audience engagement, and community development, connecting with relevant audiences and networks.

Media Partner

Provides editorial coverage, interviews, promotion, and audience reach across print, digital, and broadcast.

Technology Partner

Provides technical expertise, digital infrastructure, platforms, software, and tools.

Venue & Hospitality Partner

Provides space, facilities, accommodation, catering, and guest services for events and programs.

Sponsor

Provides financial support, resources, or services that help deliver the platform and its annual gathering.

Speakers & Contributors

Individuals who share expertise, guide conversations, lead workshops, mentor, or volunteer their time.

With gratitude

This is yours as much as ours.

KoD would not exist without the belief, vision, and sustained support of HRH Princess Noura bint Saud bin Nayef bin Abdulaziz Al Saud.

As founder of both Rukun and AlMashtal, she created the conditions that made this possible. Her understanding of what design can do as a system of knowledge has informed every decision from the beginning.

The platform was founded by Brandie Janow, who leads its vision, curation, research, and partnerships, in the conviction that design, science, and the pursuit of knowledge belong in the same room.

And to everyone who has ever taken part, the speakers who opened their thinking, the contributors and partners who gave their time, and every person who pulled up a chair to listen and join in: thank you. This is yours as much as ours.
