Education

Design Education and Its Future

What should design students learn when artificial intelligence can generate images, text, code, and rapid proposals?

The initial research was written independently for an advisory discussion at Iowa State University. It considered AI and machine learning alongside computational design, ethics, research, cultural understanding, STEAM, business skills, and industry relationships. Those subjects remain relevant, but the availability of generative AI has changed what students can produce and what educators need to assess.

The question is no longer simply whether students use AI. It is how they verify results, develop independent judgment, explain their decisions, and understand what a tool cannot tell them. A recent advisory discussion also considered the choices individual professors make about AI in their classrooms. That discussion is a subject for further examination, not a statement of university policy or an adopted curriculum.

Research by Brandie Janow · Inaugural member, Iowa State University Industrial Design Advisory Board (2024)
