About

Design as a system of knowledge

Knowledge of Design is a global initiative dedicated to advancing design as a field of knowledge.

Grounded in science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics, it examines how design informs systems, environments, decisions, and how we live. Its record spans research, publications, workshops, partnerships, annual editions, and public programming.

The archive keeps that material accessible beyond a single gathering, preserving the people, questions, evidence, and research that have accumulated through the initiative.
