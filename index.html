Knowledge of Design

Knowledge of Design advances the study and understanding of design, exploring its relationships with science, technology, engineering, arts, mathematics, culture, and everyday life. Its work includes research, publications, workshops, partnerships, annual editions, and public programming.

Featured research publication

Making a Design Culture

A Critical Dialogue on Agency, Experimentation, and Material Capacity

Developed from a critical research dialogue with Harry Dobbs and Paulo Pimentel, the paper investigates the research, experimentation and technical expertise involved in developing a design culture, technical confidence and lasting public value.

A design culture gains depth when people can direct ideas, investigate possibilities, translate knowledge into material form, engage the public, and build expertise over time.
