Knowledge of Design

KoD is a global initiative dedicated to advancing design as a field of knowledge. Grounded in science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics, it examines how design informs systems, environments, decisions, and how we live. Its record spans research, publications, workshops, partnerships, annual editions, and public programming.

The work of Knowledge of Design

From inquiry to shared knowledge

Research, critical exchange and documentation form a continuous body of work. Questions developed through conversations, field observations and professional practice inform further investigation, publications and public programming.

Inquiry

Investigate

Frame questions, gather evidence and examine how design influences people, systems and everyday life.

Exchange

Connect

Bring different disciplines and perspectives into conversation through talks, workshops and collaborations.

Continuity

Document

Preserve methods, ideas and research records so they remain available for study, critique and future work.

Recent research brings together focused studies and longer-running inquiries, including Making a Design Culture and Design Ecosystems.
