Why KoD exists

KoD examines the knowledge, decisions, skills, and resources behind design. Its research and public programs bring together perspectives from science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics, with attention to the people, materials, and environments affected by what is made.

Read original research, explore past editions and sessions, and follow the questions contributors have raised about education, experimentation, production, and everyday life.
