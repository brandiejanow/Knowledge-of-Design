Knowledge of Design

KoD is a global initiative dedicated to advancing design as a field of knowledge. Grounded in science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics, it examines how design informs systems, environments, decisions, and how we live. Its record spans research, publications, workshops, partnerships, annual editions, and public programming.

Featured research publication

Making a Design Culture

A Critical Dialogue on Agency, Experimentation, and Material Capacity

Developed from a critical research dialogue with Harry Dobbs and Paulo Pimentel, the paper investigates the conditions that help design cultures develop their own practice, technical confidence and lasting public value.

A design culture gains depth when people can direct ideas, investigate possibilities, translate knowledge into material form, engage the public, and build expertise over time.
